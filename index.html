03.Case study

// EY · DigiGST 2.0 · Apache Pulsar on AKS

From 37.75 TPS and failing consumers to 820 TPS with zero errors

GST filings arrive in huge bursts around deadlines. The streaming layer was topping out below 40 transactions per second and every consumer was failing under load. I rebuilt the Pulsar topology, tuned brokers, bookies and consumers, and re-sized AKS node pools — then load-tested it step by step to 820 TPS with a clean error log.
